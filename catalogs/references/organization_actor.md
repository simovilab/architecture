# Organization and Actor Catalog Documentation



### Definition According to TOGAF

The purpose of the Organization/Actor catalog is to capture a definitive listing of all participants that interact with IT, including users and owners of IT systems. The catalog can be referenced when developing requirements in order to test for completeness.

### Relevant Questions

This catalog is designed to provide clear answers to essential operational and organizational questions, such as:

* Who are the key organizations involved in public transportation system development and operation?
* What are the specific roles and responsibilities of each organization?
* Who are the key individuals (actors) responsible for system implementation and operation?
* How do organizations relate to each other hierarchically?
